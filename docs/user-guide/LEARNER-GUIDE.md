# Learner Guide: Getting Started with KubeLab

## 1. Navigating KubeLab

1. **Learn**: Browse 12 curriculum tracks from Linux foundations to multi-cluster SRE.
2. **Labs**: Launch disposable sandboxes and solve real-world cloud-native challenges.
3. **Practice**: Take adaptive quizzes, timed challenges, and flashcard drills.
4. **Incidents**: Enter the Production Incident Simulator to troubleshoot live broken systems.
5. **Skill Tree**: Visualize your mastery level (0-5) across the cloud-native DAG.
6. **Certifications**: Earn verifiable digital credentials by completing hands-on timed practical exams.
